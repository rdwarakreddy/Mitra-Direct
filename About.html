The Problem We Solve

Avoid high marketplace costs.

Standard delivery apps take large percentage cuts from every sale. They often hide local store listings behind paid advertisements, which makes organic business growth very difficult.

Mitra Direct serves as a simple toolkit alternative. We connect local creators directly with nearby buyers so neighbor networks can run free of extra delivery fees.
